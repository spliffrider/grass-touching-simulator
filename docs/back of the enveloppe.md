The Grass Guru welcomes you. He is Sensi, quest giver, lore teller, tutorial goblin-but-human-energy. He knows too much, but also absolutely does not have his story straight.
The player is the new Toucher of the ancient grass. 
The ancient grass has a lifebar:
Touching it restores life.
Time drains it.
The drain gets worse as the Scourge grows/game lasts longer. 
When you can’t keep up anymore, the run ends and you get meta progression. 
The meta progression has skill unlocks that make the player's grass touching powers better in several ways, so that he/she can keep the grass alive for longer next run.
